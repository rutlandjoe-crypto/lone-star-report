## Editorial Standard

Journalistic integrity is the No. 1 rule.

Use credible straight-news reporting and authoritative primary sources.
No opinion content, low-quality blogs, filler, unsupported claims, or invented facts.

Coverage must be genuinely statewide:
Amarillo, Lubbock, El Paso, Laredo, Rio Grande Valley, Corpus Christi,
San Antonio, Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, Beaumont, East Texas,
and communities across the Great State of Texas.
